A transparent screening companion to the country-risk work in Artemis Forecasting PR #2. It turns selected open indicators into a 0–100 risk score, then shows exactly where the snapshot is incomplete.

How the method is designed

The design borrows the legible logic of commercial country-risk systems without claiming their data or calibration. Governance carries 50%, macroeconomics 25%, and civic freedom 25% when all groups are present. Missing groups are excluded and the available weights are renormalised.

What it can — and cannot — validate

Use the index as a disciplined cross-check on a qualitative country judgment: does the direction look plausible, which component drives the disagreement, and what missing evidence should be collected next? It can challenge overconfident narrative, make assumptions explicit, and provide a repeatable baseline for later snapshots.

It cannot replace analyst judgment. The score does not model conflict exposure, sanctions, external financing, commodity concentration, exchange-rate stress, institutional nuance, or event timing. The weights and min/max mappings are policy choices, not empirically calibrated probabilities. WGI and Freedom House are perception and expert-coded measures with their own coverage and revisions. A “very high” band is a screening signal, not a forecast.
