A composite country risk index

A screening companion to the country-risk work in Artemis Forecasting. It uses selected open indicators to produce a 0–100 risk score and shows where the snapshot is incomplete.

Read this before the number: The method follows a structure similar to ICRG/PRS, but does not use licensed ICRG or PRS data. In the 2024 snapshot, IMF and World Bank WGI components could not be obtained and are marked missing. Each country's score therefore rests on the available Freedom House component alone. No missing value is imputed.

How the method is designed

The method follows the structure of commercial country-risk systems but does not use their data or claim their calibration. Governance carries 50%, macroeconomics 25%, and civic freedom 25% when all groups are present. Missing groups are excluded and the available weights are renormalised.

How to use the index

Use the index as a disciplined cross-check on a qualitative country judgment: does the direction look plausible, which component drives the disagreement, and what missing evidence should be collected next? It can challenge overconfident narrative, make assumptions explicit, and provide a repeatable baseline for later snapshots.

It cannot replace analyst judgment. The score does not model conflict exposure, sanctions, external financing, commodity concentration, exchange-rate stress, institutional nuance, or event timing. The weights and min/max mappings are policy choices, not empirically calibrated probabilities. WGI and Freedom House are perception and expert-coded measures with their own coverage and revisions. A “very high” band is a screening signal, not a forecast.

Sources and limitations
The data files and source list are available in Artemis Forecasting. The snapshot was generated for target year 2024; provider revisions may change a rerun. The repository records that some IMF requests were refused and that the affected WGI observations were unavailable. Those fields remain blank.
