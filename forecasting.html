What Artemis records

Each report import records a SHA-256 fingerprint of the report, its source URL, title and date, retrieval time, software version, and assumptions. Importing the same report again does not create a duplicate. Drafts stay separate from approved forecasts and are not included in the results.

Drafts are excluded from Brier scores, calibration analysis, win rate, leaderboard rankings, and Good Judgment Open comparisons until a human review process promotes them. Validated or human-entered forecasts are the only records that can contribute to those measures.
