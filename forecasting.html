What Artemis records

Each report import stores a SHA-256 report snapshot, source URL, title and date, retrieval time, generator version, and assumptions. Re-importing an identical report is idempotent. Drafts live separately from approved forecasts so the analytics layer remains honest.

Drafts are excluded from Brier scores, calibration analysis, win rate, leaderboard rankings, and Good Judgment Open comparisons until a human review workflow promotes them. Validated or human-entered forecasts are the only records that can contribute to those measures.
